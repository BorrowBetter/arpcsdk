Regenerate against FDR's ARPC spec 2026.21.0

- New `getApplicationStatus()` — `GET /v1/application/{id}/status`, a flat field-projected record (`?query=`).
- `patchLead()` accepts `draft_type` and `first_draft_date`.
- New fields: `EligibilityApplicant.social_security_number`, `DraRequest.credit_utilization`, `LeadDebtAccount.reference_id`, and `ineligible_reason` (`ProgramIneligibilityReason[]`) on eligibility and lead applications.
- **Breaking (types):** `IneligibleReason` is renamed `DebtAccountIneligibilityReason`. Array items such as `PaymentScheduleOption` and `LeadCondition` are no longer nullable, and `LeadApplicant` itself is non-null (`co_applicant` stays nullable).
- The spec moved to OpenAPI 3.1. A new `nullable-refs` repair keeps `error_details`, `bank_details`, `co_applicant`, `readiness` and `credit_pull_consent` typed `T | null`; nullable fields also now generate named aliases (e.g. `LeadApplicationBankDetails`).
- Dropped the `select-program-error-responses` repair: FDR fixed it upstream.
